# Changelog

# NIRUX II 0.2.0

The whole game: seven stages, the Reaper's side, NULL and the ending.

- Stage 3, The Archive Cathedral: a climb up the nave under stained glass,
  ghost files, a hidden way over the crossing, and the Choir of the Deleted
  (three singing heads that fall one by one, a chorus of notes all round).
- Stage 4, The Cron Tower: clockwork ledges that come and go on the tick
  and flicker before they go, pendulums that cannot be destroyed, the bells
  twice, and the Clockwork Warden (rolling gears, leaps and shock waves).
- Stage 5, The Frozen Cache: slippery ice, icicles that fall as you pass,
  freezing water, a hidden way, and the Frost Wyrm (cracks show where it
  breaks out; it arcs over the arena and breathes ice).
- Stage 6, The Kernel Crypt: lava, crumbling bridges, daemon knights, and
  the Reaper, who walks with NIRUX for a while and reaps what NULL raised;
  then the Daemon Lord (fire rain, dives, its daemons).
- Stage 7, NULL's Keep: everything at once, then NULL in three phases:
  bursts and teleports, its fragments firing all round, sweeps of static to
  duck and jump.
- The ending: the Reaper takes what is left of NULL, ECHO speaks clear
  again, and the credits name the stages and the fallen.
- ECHO's briefings break up more the deeper NIRUX goes; NULL and the Reaper
  speak along the way.
- Seven new songs: Cathedral, Clockwork, Frost, Kernel, The Keep, Null and
  Home.
- The autopilot test now clears all seven stages and their bosses on Easy
  and Normal, and every stage is checked to be walkable.

# NIRUX II 0.1.0

The first two stages of NIRUX II - NIGHT OF THE DAEMONS.

- A gothic action platformer in the C64 style: run, jump (a firm arc, a
  short hop on a tap), climb ladders, drop through ledges, fire ahead or
  upwards.
- NIRUX from the first game, now seen from the side: the scarf is the life
  and is drawn as long as it is, the visor is a lamp that shows hidden
  ledges and holds ghosts.
- The prologue picks up where the first game ended; a hero who beat the
  first game is welcomed back with an extra life.
- Stage 1, The Process Graveyard: rain and lightning, graves the dead rise
  from, a mausoleum, the chapel where the cron bells ring, a crumbling
  bridge, iron spikes, the Reaper on the last hill, and the Gravedigger.
- Stage 2, The Swap Marsh: fog, black water, logs that sink, a hidden way
  only the lamp shows, ghost files, the stilt village, a knight, fork
  bombs, the bells again, and the Bog Daemon on its islands.
- Four weapons (bolt, hammer, disc, whip) with three levels each, shrines
  that mend the scarf and mark where you come back, gems (a life for every
  hundred).
- ECHO briefs every stage, its signal breaking up deeper down; NULL taunts
  in red.
- Eight new songs: Daemons, Descent, Graveyard, Marsh, The Bells Toll,
  Deeper, To Be Continued and Signal Lost.
- Gamepads, a Controls screen, Easy, Normal and Hard, level select, high
  scores, sharp scaling on any screen, all from the first game's engine.
- Tests prove every stage can be walked and that the autopilot clears both
  stages and their bosses.
