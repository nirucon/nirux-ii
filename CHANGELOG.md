# NIRUX II 0.1.0

The first two stages of NIRUX II - NIGHT OF THE DAEMONS.

- A gothic action platformer in the C64 style: run, jump (a firm arc, a
  short hop on a tap), climb ladders, drop through ledges, fire ahead or
  upwards.
- NIRUX from the first game, now seen from the side: the scarf is the life
  and is drawn as long as it is, the visor is a lamp that shows hidden
  ledges and holds ghosts.
- The prologue picks up where the first game ended; a hero who beat the
  first game is welcomed back with an extra life.
- Stage 1, The Process Graveyard: rain and lightning, graves the dead rise
  from, a mausoleum, the chapel where the cron bells ring, a crumbling
  bridge, iron spikes, the Reaper on the last hill, and the Gravedigger.
- Stage 2, The Swap Marsh: fog, black water, logs that sink, a hidden way
  only the lamp shows, ghost files, the stilt village, a knight, fork
  bombs, the bells again, and the Bog Daemon on its islands.
- Four weapons (bolt, hammer, disc, whip) with three levels each, shrines
  that mend the scarf and mark where you come back, gems (a life for every
  hundred).
- ECHO briefs every stage, its signal breaking up deeper down; NULL taunts
  in red.
- Eight new songs: Daemons, Descent, Graveyard, Marsh, The Bells Toll,
  Deeper, To Be Continued and Signal Lost.
- Gamepads, a Controls screen, Easy, Normal and Hard, level select, high
  scores, sharp scaling on any screen, all from the first game's engine.
- Tests prove every stage can be walked and that the autopilot clears both
  stages and their bosses.
